Teacher guide

Keep all 20 students involved

  1. Make four mixed-age teams. Give students numbers 1–5.
  2. Use quiet huddles. Teams discuss before their numbered speaker answers.
  3. Protect younger voices. Let them suggest concrete words; older students explain or use them in sentences.
  4. Rotate every round. The screen tells you which speaker should answer.
  5. Stop shouting. Only the current numbered speaker can give the final answer.
