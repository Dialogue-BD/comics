The visual world and production

A shared motion studio

Deep green, warm paper, mint, gold and blue. Abstract people, records, homes, a chip and a growing plant recur across the films. Simple objects assemble, connect, write, balance and grow as the speaker’s ideas change.

The illustrations express each person’s questions, worries and hopes. They do not predict what AI will do.

Original audio, aligned words

The original nine recordings are preserved. Local speech recognition supplied word timings; scene boundaries follow spoken phrases. Each film uses three scenes, restrained camera movement and a stable caption area.

Exports: 1280 × 720, 24 fps, H.264 video with AAC audio. Audio is normalized for the film exports. There is no added music or sound effect. The activity uses the original MP3s.

The live activity and exports share one deterministic Canvas renderer. Live transcripts support phrase replay, word replay, vocabulary meanings and reduced motion.
