AI fluency, listening & critical thinking · 25–40 minutes

Listen. Notice. Understand.
Everyone has something to say.

Every step is available from the start. Follow these suggestions or jump straight to the words, animated transcript, or discussion.

  1. Hear the person. Listen to the whole recording, then choose the main idea. In pairs, let one person control the audio and the other explain the idea.
  2. Catch the words. Listen to a section and tap only the words you hear. Check your words, then continue to the next section. Replay and review the meanings whenever you need to.
  3. Watch the meaning. Open the transcript whenever you like. Watch the pictures move with the speaker’s words — each key word appears at the moment you hear it. Tap a word or transcript phrase to replay it at natural speed. The pictures illustrate the person’s questions and hopes.
  4. Think together. Share a view, give a reason, and ask your partner a follow-up question. Different opinions are welcome.

Projector theatre → Play any of the nine voices full-screen, with live animated pictures and big word-by-word captions — made for the front of the room.

Swap roles for the next voice. Choose three voices for a shorter activity, or explore all nine over several sessions.

How the challenge adapts

Everyone starts with more support. Two strong rounds add a challenge. A difficult round adds support. Replay and hesitation never cost points. Students can choose a challenge themselves.

Each level uses the same sections of about eleven seconds. More support asks you to catch up to two words, Balanced up to four, and Stretch up to six. Harder challenges also add word choices. Speech is always at its original speed. This is practice feedback, not a language-level test.

Projector view enlarges the activity for a shared screen. Let teams discuss choices before checking them; use the group score as a shared goal.
