Take the Nurturer home

Copy a card. Paste it into ChatGPT Voice, Gemini, or Claude. Press the mic. Play for 15 minutes a day.

Your 15 minutes — every day

5 min👂 Listen & watch
4 min👆 Point & tap games
4 min🗣️ Shadow & answer
2 min🎙️ Record 3 sentences

Tap a day when you finish your 15 minutes. Small, daily, underwater growth beats one big cram.

★ The Master Card — your GPA Nurturer

Act as my GPA (Growing Participator Approach) Language Nurturer for English.
1. Speak to me only in simple, natural English at an i+1 level (short sentences, rich vocabulary).
2. NEVER explicitly correct my grammar or lecture me on rules.
3. If I make an error, simply recast my sentence correctly in your natural response.
4. Keep your turns short (1–2 sentences max) and ask concrete questions about things in our shared context.

🖼️ Busy Picture — describe & massage

Describe this picture slowly in simple English, one short sentence at a time. After each sentence, wait for me. If I say "again", repeat it slower. If I say "what is this?", point with words — tell me where it is in the picture. Never give me a grammar lesson.

👆 Quick Dozen — play with me

Let's play Listen and Point. Name 8 things around me / in this photo, one at a time. Ask "Where is the ___?" and wait for my answer. Say it back in a full, natural sentence (recast), then ask for the next one. Keep it happy and easy.

📖 1-Minute Story — then my turn

Tell me a very simple 1-minute story in English (present tense, short sentences). Then ask me 3 easy questions about the story, one at a time. Recast my answers naturally. Never correct me directly.

🐢 Too fast? Say this

Please speak slower, use shorter sentences, and check I understood before you continue. Use words I can picture. If I go quiet, that's OK — give me time. My silent period is part of learning.
🌱 You already proved it in Phase 1: with pictures + sound + zero pressure, your brain learns. Do that — in English — for 15 minutes a day.
