Implicit Recasting (No Red Pens)

How conversational AI repairs errors organically
🗣️ Student Says: "Boy run road."
❌ Traditional Correction
"Incorrect grammar! Missing auxiliary verb 'is' and preposition. Say: The boy is running down the road."
Spikes anxiety; shuts down communication.
💚 AI Nurturer Recast
"Ah, yes! The young boy is running down the busy road! Where is he headed?"
Validates meaning + Models natural syntax.
