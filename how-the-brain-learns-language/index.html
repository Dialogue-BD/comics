00:00 – 04:00 · Comprehensible Input vs. Noise

The Immersion Shock

A language you have never heard. No words on the wall. No translation.

Teacher Cue Ask students to press A first and try to pick the right picture. When they guess and feel confused, explain: "Without picture context, any new language is just noise." Then press B and watch the pointing hand connect sound directly to meaning!
✦ ✦ ✦
Press A. Just listen. Try to guess. Guessing is the point.
🔇 Sound + NO picture = NOISE
Your brain cannot hold it. It feels stressful. That feeling has a name: incomprehensible input.
👁️ Sound + picture + pointing = MEANING
Same sound. Now your brain locks it in — with zero translation. That is Comprehensible Input.

16:00 – 22:00 · GPA · The AI as Nurturer · No red pens

Your pocket Nurturer

A good Nurturer never says “wrong.” She just says it beautifully — and you absorb it.

Teacher Cue Walk through the 3 core AI Nurturer games: (1) Quick Dozen Listen & Point, (2) Implicit Recasting where broken learner phrases are reformulated warmly without a red pen, and (3) Busy Picture Story Massaging.

Game 1 — Quick Dozen: Listen & Point (GPA Phase 1)

Listen. Tap what you hear. That's it. Comprehension first — speaking comes later.

☆ ☆ ☆ ☆ ☆ ☆
Press Start. The Nurturer will ask you to find something.

Game 2 — Say it your way. Hear it grow. (Implicit Recasting)

Tap a learner sentence. Listen to what a Nurturer does — no correction, just a natural recast.

Nurturer (Emma)Welcome! I will never mark you wrong. I will play, point, and say things in a way you can understand — at your level, plus one small step. That step is called i+1: 95% familiar, 5% new.

Tap any part of the market scene (or the buttons) to hear the Nurturer describe it clearly in context.

22:00 – 25:00 · Your Daily Protocol

Take the Nurturer home

Copy a card. Paste it into ChatGPT Voice, Gemini, or Claude. Press the mic. Play for 15 minutes a day.

Teacher Cue Have students pull out their phones right now, tap Copy this prompt on the Master Card, and paste it into ChatGPT Voice or Gemini Live so they leave the lecture with a working personal GPA Nurturer!

Your 15 minutes — every day

5 min👂 Listen & watch
4 min👆 Point & tap games
4 min🗣️ Shadow & answer
2 min🎙️ Record 3 sentences

Tap a day when you finish your 15 minutes. Small, daily, underwater growth beats one big cram.

★ The Master Card — your GPA Nurturer

Act as my GPA (Growing Participator Approach) Language Nurturer for English.
1. Speak to me only in simple, natural English at an i+1 level (short sentences, rich vocabulary).
2. NEVER explicitly correct my grammar or lecture me on rules.
3. If I make an error, simply recast my sentence correctly in your natural response.
4. Keep your turns short (1–2 sentences max) and ask concrete questions about things in our shared context.

🖼️ Busy Picture — describe & massage

Describe this picture slowly in simple English, one short sentence at a time. After each sentence, wait for me. If I say "again", repeat it slower. If I say "what is this?", point with words — tell me where it is in the picture. Never give me a grammar lesson.

👆 Quick Dozen — play with me

Let's play Listen and Point. Name 8 things around me / in this photo, one at a time. Ask "Where is the ___?" and wait for my answer. Say it back in a full, natural sentence (recast), then ask for the next one. Keep it happy and easy.

📖 1-Minute Story — then my turn

Tell me a very simple 1-minute story in English (present tense, short sentences). Then ask me 3 easy questions about the story, one at a time. Recast my answers naturally. Never correct me directly.

🐢 Too fast? Say this

Please speak slower, use shorter sentences, and check I understood before you continue. Use words I can picture. If I go quiet, that's OK — give me time. My silent period is part of learning.
🌱 You already proved it in Phase 1: with pictures + sound + zero pressure, your brain learns. Do that — in English — for 15 minutes a day.
