# Rajshahi problem cards — first 25 data review

**Status:** The first 25 cards are built as a separate activity. This file preserves the data review and source notes; the card manifests, artwork, web activity, and print files are in this folder.

**Home:** A separate activity alongside Picture This!. Cards 1–6 are the topics in the supplied brief; cards 7–25 are researched proposals, including the two household experiences you described. The scenes are discussion scenarios, so they do not assert that every depicted incident happened exactly as shown.

**Grant challenge on every back:** 30 days, a 60-second pitch, and one small pilot. The proposed amount and pilot scope vary by card. Amounts are classroom discussion constraints, not procurement estimates. Avoid major construction and police crackdowns; name who will maintain the pilot.

**Tile order:** 1–2 physical or spatial realities; 3–4 human habits or behaviors; 5–6 structural or economic drivers. Tapping a label reveals the two collocations listed below.

**Question order:** Observe → narrate → distinguish → diagnose → anticipate → propose.
