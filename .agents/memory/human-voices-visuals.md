---
name: Human Voices listening visuals
description: Teaching purpose and fidelity rules for animations in the Human Voices on AI activity
---

Use the animations as comprehensible-input support for ESL students: watching
people, actions, relationships, and contrasts should help explain the speaker's
meaning, rather than merely decorate the recording with related objects.

**Why:** The user wants students to better follow and understand what the person
is speaking about by watching the video.

**How to apply:** Tie visual changes to the actual spoken ideas. Preserve the
distinction between a speaker's question, concern, or hope and an established
fact; do not imply that hypothetical AI benefits have happened. Keep live scenes
and downloadable films consistent, and preserve the original voices and timing
when changing the visual support.
