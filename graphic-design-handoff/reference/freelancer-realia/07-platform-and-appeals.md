# Platform & appeals

**Voice:** Level-2 Fiverr seller (1.5 years, no prior violations), GetHuman complaint archive, 2026
**The words (paraphrased):** Account disabled over an alleged outside-payment attempt he denies; appeal feels like shouting into a machine.

### The Betopia blast radius

**Voice:** Prothom Alo (English), "Fiverr accounts manipulation by Betopia puts country's freelancing sector in crisis," Aug 2026
**The words (paraphrased):** Betopia harvested NID cards and passport details from thousands of jobseekers, opened Fiverr accounts it secretly controlled, and rigged rankings with fake orders. Late July: ~300 accounts suspended, thousands more under monitoring, ~$1.5M in work orders cancelled or frozen.
**Context:** The cheats deserved it — but legitimate freelancers now live in fear the next detection sweep takes their account for no reason.
**Classroom use:** "Your profile is a storefront, not your house" — the case for direct-client capability.
