# Chasing payment

Getting the money home — fees, markups, stuck withdrawals, and the business math nobody teaches.

### The public "scam alert"

**Voice:** Mahabur Rahman, public Facebook post with WhatsApp screenshots, Sep 10, 2026
**The words (paraphrased):** Slide-by-slide callout of a client who received the delivered PDF then went quiet: repeated "when will you pay?", seven unanswered voice calls, pleading Bengali messages warning that silence destroys his local reputation — and a polarized comment section arguing fraud versus a "fake PDF."
**Context:** Payment dispute as public theatre: with no escrow and no leverage, the freelancer's only collection tool is naming-and-shaming — and it may burn the very reputation he's trying to protect.
**Classroom use:** Map the exits: what a freelancer writes *before* delivery (deposit terms, milestone language) so chasing never becomes a public feud.

### The vanishing 290 taka

**Voice:** Mahajabin Islam, "Payoneer Bangladesh" Facebook group, June 29, 2026
**The words (paraphrased):** The app showed 122.43 taka per dollar. She withdrew $45 — about 5,509 taka by that rate. What arrived: 5,221.82. Nearly 290 taka gone with no receipt, because Payoneer's up-to-3.5% conversion markup hides inside the exchange rate.
**Context:** Commenters just nodded — the app shows the gross rate, then quietly deducts before payout. She learned it by coming up short; nobody taught her to expect it.
**Classroom use:** Trace a real $100 payout through fees to taka in hand; then reprice a quote backwards from the taka you need.

### A third of twelve dollars

**Voice:** Sher Ali, "Payoneer Bangladesh" Facebook group, Sep 27, 2026
**The words (paraphrased):** $12 Adobe Stock payout via Skrill; $4 lost to the withdrawal fee — a third of the whole thing. Thread becomes community Skrill-vs-Payoneer math.
**Context:** Small payouts get eaten alive; the group does collectively what no course teaches — fee arithmetic.
**Classroom use:** "What's the smallest payout worth withdrawing?" — a real worksheet problem.

### High income, unpredictable income

**Voice:** UI/UX designer quoted in the Financial Express, "The banking gap standing between freelancers and their own money," Sep 28, 2026
**The words (paraphrased):** You can have a high-income month without having a predictable income — and the fees shave 3–5% off every single payment, an estimated $75–125 million a year across the sector, on money already earned.
**Context:** First-person voices (Atik, Shimanto, Hridoy, Mahin, Rabita) on exchange-rate surprises and treating the all-in loss as a cost of doing business.
**Classroom use:** Pricing backwards from a target taka figure — the "Shimanto method."

### The name-match wall

**Voice:** Arif Hossain, "Help Bangladesh Fiverr" Facebook group, June 28, 2026
**The words (paraphrased):** Can't withdraw Payoneer to another person's bank account — the name-match rule blocks it; earlier funds were removed after a name mismatch.
**Context:** The fine print that bites: account names must match across platform, Payoneer, and bank, or money gets stuck.
**Classroom use:** Reading a fee/schedule page before signing up — the most boring and most valuable lesson.
