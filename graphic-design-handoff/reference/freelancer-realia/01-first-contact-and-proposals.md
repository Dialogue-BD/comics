# First contact & proposals

The cover letter, the first message, the bid — where work is won or lost before it starts.

### The template everyone sends

**Voice:** Mst Taniya Akter Mina, trainee at E-learning & Earning Ltd (Rajbari), Facebook group post, July 8, 2026
**The words:**
> "I have reviewed your project and can complete it professionally with high quality and on-time delivery. I'm ready to start immediately."
**Context:** Her institute's proposal-template exercise, posted proudly in a freelancer group. Nothing about the client's actual project — the exact letter every other beginner sends, and every client skims past.
**Classroom use:** Hand students this letter next to a real job post; ask them to spot everything it doesn't answer.

### Skip the marketplace

**Voice:** Ash Senpai, 19-year-old Bangladeshi brand designer (~83K followers), Facebook reel, Aug 13, 2026
**The words (paraphrased from reel):** Stop using Fiverr/Upwork for a first client — post spec work in niche, client-rich communities until inbound inquiries come.
**Context:** Commenters piled in sharing their own Fiverr struggles and weak-English anxiety. The thread reads as a generation routing around the proposal wall entirely.
**Classroom use:** Debate prompt — when is the marketplace the wrong first move?

### Why generic dies

**Voice:** dev.to guide, "How to Write Your First Upwork Proposal," Oct 2026
**The words (paraphrased):** Generic proposals fail because clients read only the first lines; the hook must echo the job post's specifics.
**Context:** Corroborates what the freelancers show by negative example — Tania's template never quotes the client back.
**Classroom use:** The "mirror the client's words" rule, taught as a line-editing exercise.

### Sound real, not impressive

**Voice:** Suraj Mahapatra, Medium, Aug 2026
**Link:** https://medium.com/@surajmahapatra736/i-sent-the-same-fiverr-proposal-17-times-the-17th-one-got-me-my-first-client-8348ad1010a5
**The words:**
> "I stopped trying to sound impressive. Instead, I tried to sound real."
**Context:** He sent the same Fiverr proposal 17 times; the 17th won his first client. The winning version stopped listing skills and claiming expertise — it acknowledged he was new, said he'd read their requirements, explained his plan, and thanked them for considering someone with zero reviews. "It simply removed enough doubt for one buyer to take a chance."
**Classroom use:** Rewrite drill — students take a braggy template and convert it into an honest, specific first message.

### 23 days of bidding for $5

**Voice:** Bappi Ahammed, Kushtia, interviewed by Prothom Alo, circa 2023
**Link:** http://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/good-day-bangladesh/fau43smmtu
**The words (paraphrased from reporting):** His first Fiverr job paid $5 and came after 23 days of bidding; the US client tipped $10 and handed him four more jobs worth $200.
**Context:** Learned freelancing from YouTube; worked sorting betel leaves at Tk 300/day for three months to buy a used laptop, then studied 10–15 hours a day for nine months. Now earns about $5,000/month across 500+ Fiverr jobs.
**Classroom use:** Normalize the timeline — the first client is a numbers game plus reliability, not a talent lottery. Bangladeshi voice, close to home.

### Productive volume

**Voice:** "Mar," interviewed on mujiburrahman.com, Sept 2026
**Link:** https://mujiburrahman.com/how-she-landed-an-8000-upwork-client-in-7-days-with-no-testimonials/
**The words (paraphrased):** In one two-hour session she examined 111 jobs but submitted proposals to only seven; her first client came around proposal 23.
**Context:** The interviewer's lesson: "One proposal is an event. Fifty proposals become a dataset." The method is research each job, qualify hard, and customize — most of the work is choosing, not writing.
**Classroom use:** Teach job triage before proposal writing — the "111 examined, 7 sent" ratio as a selection exercise.

### Say what you'll do on day one

**Voice:** famoustiger0808, dev.to, circa Jan 2026
**Link:** https://dev.to/famoustiger0808/from-zero-to-my-first-3-upwork-clients-as-a-full-stack-engineer-1hco
**The words (paraphrased):** "I wrote short, specific proposals, referenced their stack, and suggested exactly what I would do in the first day."
**Context:** Full-stack engineer whose first three Upwork clients came from unglamorous tasks — fixing a broken login, cleaning up a buggy API endpoint, building a basic CRUD interface. Small jobs became reviews, reviews became trust.
**Classroom use:** Proposal template with a mandatory "Day 1 plan" line — clients buy the plan, not the profile.

### Buy the first review

**Voice:** Naveen Soni, dev.to, Aug 2020
**Link:** https://dev.to/naveen6802/how-i-got-my-first-freelancing-job-3cfb
**The words:**

### The non-native writer who won in 30 minutes

**Voice:** Komi Benson, Medium, circa 2025
**Link:** https://medium.com/@komibenson1/the-proposal-that-got-me-my-first-writing-job-on-upwork-e4ed46b80082
**The words:**
> "I am not a native English writer. However, I am confident that my strong command of natural English… make me an excellent fit."
**Context:** Her proposal's first lines proved she'd read the brief; the client sent an offer within 30 minutes despite 10–15 competing proposals. Honesty about English plus proof of reading beat every native-speaker claim.
**Classroom use:** For Rajshahi students directly — non-native English is not the disqualifier; skimming the brief is.

### The call freeze

**Voice:** Wakil, freelance designer (Instagram reel by @stickoo_studio), Oct 7, 2026
**Link:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeMvxdyAYBy/
**The words:**
> "You know that feeling? The client finally wants to meet and suddenly you forget every English word you know."
**Context:** A new client wants a Google Meet; Wakil's excitement collapses into panic before the call, and he hunts for an English-translator service. The call itself goes fine — the fear was the real obstacle.
**Classroom use:** Open the Discovery Call Drill with this line — the fear is universal; preparation is the cure.

### Text only, no calls

**Voice:** Commenter on Kaosar's Facebook post, Sep 25, 2026
**Link:** https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=pfbid02gT2f4wEg8vMC8QrE5MAXWXBJPXoq5NRHnG4wKAiYhdaQGbf6q3Px7jmHrvg2EBuKl&id=61590893181460

> "ami bhai contact no dei na text er upor text" — I don't give my contact number, text only.
