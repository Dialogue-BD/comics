# AI displacement chats

Clients leaving for AI — in their own chat screenshots.

### "Nah man, Claude can do all that"

**Voice:** Toufic Ahmed Khan, young web developer, Facebook post, Sep 17, 2026 (caption: "Ai Effect 💔💔💔")
**The words (chat screenshot):**
> "Hey, anything in pipeline for me I can help?"
> "Nah man i make my own landers now with claude, unless you've got other things."
> *(offers coding, UI/UX, design)*
> "Nah man claude can do all that."
**Context:** 32 comments of fellow freelancers sharing identical losses. One wrote his regular client of three years left the same way — the work is "done by AI" now.
**Classroom use:** The hardest role-play: the client isn't angry, just done needing you. What do you say next?

### The veteran's collapse

**Voice:** Nasir Uddin (shared via Rakibul Islam Mayshan), Facebook, Sep 13, 2026
**The words (paraphrased):** Claims $850,000 in lifetime Upwork earnings; says work volume is down 80–90%. Spent $2,000 on Connects last month and won nothing.
**Context:** Corroborated in comments. When even the top of the ladder is falling, it's not a skills problem — it's a market problem.
**Classroom use:** "Sell the outcome, not the task" — proposal language for repositioning from order-taker to advisor.

### Ten to fifteen developers

**Voice:** Mohammad Arif, Facebook post, Sep 25, 2026
**The words (paraphrased):** 10 to 15 developers he knows personally have already lost their jobs; bitter jokes about a "web development course in six seconds"; exhaustion at endless re-learning.
**Context:** The entry-level rungs disappearing first — the same rungs a generation of Bangladeshi freelancers climbed in on.
**Classroom use:** Honest framing for students: what survives is judgment, taste, and trust — things a client can't type into a prompt.

### Left behind

**Voice:** Emrazina Islam, freelancing since 2011, quoted in the Daily Star, "AI taking away basic jobs from freelancers," Sep 20, 2026
**The words:**
> "someone who does not know AI will be left behind."
**Context:** Her income fell more than 80%. Fifteen years of freelancing, and the sentence that lands is that plain.
**Classroom use:** The stakes, in one line — opens any AI-fluency session.
