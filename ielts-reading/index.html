Station 4

Keyword Detective

Before scanning, mark the keywords in the question. There are two kinds: concrete keywords — numbers, names, dates — which usually appear unchanged, and idea keywords — verbs and adjectives — which the passage will almost always paraphrase. Step through each example below.

🖥️ Worked example

Statement

The world's first public lending library that allowed anyone to borrow books for free opened in the 1850s.

Press "Find the Sentence" to reveal the passage.
Libraries have existed for thousands of years, but for most of that time they were reserved for scholars, priests, or the wealthy. Ordinary people were rarely permitted to borrow a book, let alone take one home. That changed in Manchester, England, where the Manchester Free Library opened its doors in 1852. It was the first library in the country to let any member of the public borrow books at no charge, a model that libraries across Europe and North America gradually copied over the following decades.

Does the passage agree with the statement?

Station 5

Trap Detector

Finding the right sentence is only half the job. Examiners write False and Not Given answers to sound right to a fast reader. Here are the four tricks to watch for, then a scored round to test them.

🔄 Negation Flip

The question states the exact opposite of what the passage says.

Text: "increased" → Question: "decreased"

⚖️ Qualifier Swap

A word like "some" or "most" is quietly swapped for "all" or "none".

Text: "most" → Question: "all"

🗺️ Scope Shift

A claim true for one group or place gets stretched to cover everyone, everywhere.

Text: "in coastal areas" → Question: "everywhere"

🔁 Reversed Cause & Effect

A and B are swapped, so the claim reverses what actually causes what.

Text: "A causes B" → Question: "B causes A"

👥 Pair work — Trap or True?

For each pair, find the matching idea, decide the verdict, then check. If you're wrong, the trap type is named for you.

Score: 0 / 0 answered

Text Scientists confirmed that the glacier's rate of melting has increased significantly since 2010, largely due to warmer ocean currents.

Question The glacier has been melting more slowly since 2010.

Text Although most participants reported feeling less stressed after the programme, a small number said they noticed no difference at all.

Question All participants felt less stressed after the programme.

Text In the coastal regions surveyed, plastic waste on beaches has fallen by 40 percent since the ban on single-use bags.

Question Plastic waste has fallen by 40 percent across the entire country.

Text Poor sleep has been shown to increase the risk of poor concentration the following day, according to the study's authors.

Question Poor concentration causes poor sleep, according to the study.

Text The bridge, completed in 1937, was at the time the longest suspension bridge in the world, a record it held for nearly three decades.

Question The bridge held the record for the longest suspension bridge for almost 30 years.

Text The museum's new wing, funded entirely by private donations, will house a permanent collection of twentieth-century sculpture.

Question The museum's new wing was more expensive to build than the original building.

Answer key — 1: FALSE (Negation Flip) · 2: FALSE (Qualifier Swap) · 3: NOT GIVEN (Scope Shift) · 4: FALSE (Reversed Cause & Effect) · 5: TRUE · 6: NOT GIVEN
