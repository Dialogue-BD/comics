Bangladesh Parliamentary Debate Rules & Rubric

🇧🇩 Structure (3v3 Parliamentary):

  • Government (পক্ষ দল): 1st Speaker (PM), 2nd Speaker (Minister), 3rd Speaker (Whip).
  • Opposition (বিপক্ষ দল): 1st Speaker (LO), 2nd Speaker (Deputy Leader), 3rd Speaker (Whip).
  • Reply Speeches (সমাপনী বক্তব্য): Opposition replies first, followed by Government.

✋ Points of Information (POI):

  • Allowed between 1:00 and 3:00 (1st min and last min are protected). Maximum 15 seconds.

⚖️ BDC Standard 100-Pt Rubric:

  • Logic & Analysis (35) · Evidence & Context (20) · Delivery (20) · Rebuttal & POI (15) · Team Strategy (10).
