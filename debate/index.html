Bangladesh Parliamentary Debate Rules & Rubric

🇧🇩 Structure of Bangladesh Parliamentary Debate (3v3)

  • Government (পক্ষ দল): 1st Speaker (Prime Minister), 2nd Speaker (Minister), 3rd Speaker (Whip).
  • Opposition (বিপক্ষ দল): 1st Speaker (Leader of Opp), 2nd Speaker (Deputy Leader), 3rd Speaker (Whip).
  • Reply Speeches (সমাপনী বক্তব্য): Delivered by either the 1st or 2nd speaker. Opposition replies first, followed by Government. No new arguments are allowed during reply speeches.

✋ Points of Information (POI / তথ্য জানতে চাওয়া)

  • Allowed only during substantive speeches between the 1st minute and last minute.
  • The 1st minute and final minute are Protected Time (এক মিনিট অসংরক্ষিত সময় ব্যতীত অন্য সময় সংরক্ষিত).
  • A POI must be concise (under 15 seconds) and asked in question form. The speaker on stage may accept or politely decline ("No thank you").

🔔 Timekeeper Bell Signals

  • 1 Bell at 1:00 (Protected time ends, POIs open).
  • 1 Bell at 3:00 (1 minute warning, POIs close).
  • 2 Bells at 4:00 (Time is up).
  • Continuous Bells at 4:30 (Grace period expired / overtime).

⚖️ BDC Standard 100-Point Scoring Rubric

  • Logic & Analysis (যুক্তি ও বিশ্লেষণ): 35 Points
  • Evidence & Context (উপাত্ত, প্রাসঙ্গিকতা ও তথ্য): 20 Points
  • Delivery & Demeanor (উপস্থাপন ও বাচনভঙ্গি): 20 Points
  • Rebuttal & POI (যুক্তিখণ্ডন ও পিওআই): 15 Points
  • Teamwork & Strategy (দলগত কৌশল ও সমন্বয়): 10 Points
