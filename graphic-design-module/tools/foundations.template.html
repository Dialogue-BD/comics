Design in two mediums

Fifteen pairings connect visual design with writing. Learners enter through visual or written communication; the lesson core is shared. Desktop pairs the mediums side by side. Mobile alternates them in one scroll.

Each unit has See the idea, Change a choice, and Play the redesign. Rewriting uses sentence tiles, ordering, grouping, selecting emphasis, inserting paragraph breaks, and keeping useful words. No typed response is required. Tap-to-build works on phones and keyboards; order games also support mouse drag and drop.

A2–B2 support changes the explanation. Original everyday examples form the practice bank, with short attributed authentic excerpts. Analogies link the mediums without claiming that grammar or punctuation follows literal physical laws.

Checks award practice XP once. All stages stay open. Explored records navigation, not proficiency. Saved progress and earlier awards are preserved.

The fifteen original graphic word animations are retained. Original guide artwork by Gareth David / TastyTuts is attributed and remains intact. The guide’s repetition, simplicity, and function pages have no visual example boards, so those units use interactive examples.
