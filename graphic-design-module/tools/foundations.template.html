Design in two mediums

Fifteen pairings connect visual design with writing. Learners enter through visual or written communication; the lesson core is shared. Desktop pairs the mediums side by side. Mobile alternates them in one scroll.

Each unit has See the idea, Change a choice, and Play the redesign. Every unit contains three short challenges: notice a visual feature, manipulate a design choice, and transfer the idea to a fresh case. Independent visual and written galleries show the range of each effect, with three named lab states per unit. Document templates use placeholder text to reveal form; syllables, rhyme, and meaning require real words. Rewriting uses sentence tiles, matching, ordering, sentence magnets, palette word choices, emphasis, paragraph breaks, and live word surgery. No typed response is required. Tap-to-build works on phones and keyboards; order and grouping games also support mouse drag and drop. All controls have tap and keyboard alternatives. The example galleries remain optional.

A2–B2 support changes the explanation. Original everyday examples form the practice bank, with short attributed authentic excerpts. Analogies link the mediums without claiming that grammar or punctuation follows literal physical laws.

The first two challenges award 5 practice XP each; the fresh-case challenge awards 10, once per challenge. Feedback checks the stated purpose, with more than one accepted reading where context supports it. All stages stay open. Explored records navigation, not proficiency. Saved progress and earlier awards are preserved; new challenge drafts and awards are stored separately. The finale builds a short passage and connects its organisation to its purpose.

The fifteen original graphic word animations are retained. Guide illustrations by Gareth David / TastyTuts are cropped and adapted to the paper UI, with picture captions and a footer credit. Full source pages remain available. The guide’s repetition, simplicity, and function pages have no visual example boards, so those units use interactive examples.
