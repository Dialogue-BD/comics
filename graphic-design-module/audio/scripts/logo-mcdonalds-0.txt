The supplied McDonald's mark uses two joined arches that make an M-like shape. The color is yellow, and there is very little extra detail inside the mark. We can discuss shape and color as elements, and simplicity as a principle. Familiarity may help us recognise this particular mark, so recognition alone does not prove that any similar yellow shape would work for a new business. A designer still needs to check distinctiveness, clarity, and whether the mark fits the organiser's actual audience.
