Design + English in one lesson

Explore seven graphical elements, then eight principles, then put them together in the finale. Every unit combines source examples, pictured vocabulary, an experiment, and a matched English lesson.

Use A2 for short models, B1 for connected explanations, and B2 for careful qualifications. Word parts supply clues; learners must check the picture and sentence. Greek and Latin origins are distinguished.

Checks are optional. Free speaking is not automatically graded. “Explored” records navigation, not certified proficiency. Progress and one-time practice XP stay on this device.
