Teach Through The Visual

Choose any lab. One lab usually fits 12–20 minutes; the estimates are flexible. Students can explore individually or take turns on the classroom screen.

  1. Look first (2 min). Hide the label. Ask: “What changed? What do you notice?”
  2. Name it (3 min). Tap the visual or vocabulary. Model one short sentence.
  3. Try it (5 min). Change a setting. Compare two versions. Ask which fits the client’s purpose.
  4. Explain it (3 min). Partners use the speaking frame. One is the client; one is the designer. Switch.
  5. Save the decision (2 min). Write a client message in the notebook. Mark the lab complete.

For beginners: “I chose ___ because ___.” For stronger speakers: explain a tradeoff and ask a useful question. The AI draft exercises are deliberately constructed teaching mockups, not unedited outputs from a generator.
