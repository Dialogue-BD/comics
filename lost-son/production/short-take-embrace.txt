The young man dropped to his knees. His hands were empty. He had nothing to offer.
Before he could explain, his father reached him and wrapped both arms around him.
The father held his son’s head against his face. For a moment, neither of them said anything.
Then the son began. Father, I’ve sinned against God and against you. I’m no longer worthy to be called your son anymore.
But his father was already calling to the people in the house. Help him wash. Bring him our best clothes.
The father helped him dress. He wasn’t being received as a stranger or a worker. He was being welcomed as a son.
