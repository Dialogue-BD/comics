Prepare a feast, the father said. We’re going to celebrate.
Soon, food was cooking in great pots. The courtyard filled with music and laughter.
My son was lost. Now he’s found. I thought I’d lost him forever, but he’s home.
Meanwhile, the older brother was coming back from the fields. He heard the music before he reached the house.
A boy ran out of the courtyard. Your brother’s home, he said. Your father’s having a feast because he’s come back safe.
The older brother’s face changed. A feast? For him? After everything he’s done?
