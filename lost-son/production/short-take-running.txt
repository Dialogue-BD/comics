His father was standing on the veranda, looking down the road.
Far away, a figure was coming toward the house. The father looked again.
The clothes were dirty. The shoulders were bent. But he knew that walk. It was his son.
He put down his cup without thinking about the spilled tea.
He gathered up his clothes and began to run.
He didn’t wait for his son to reach the gate. He ran out to meet him.
