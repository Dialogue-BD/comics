# AI Fluency Lab: responsible independence

## Audience and delivery
Third–fourth year university students in Rajshahi; experienced AI users; B1–B2 English. Three complete 45-minute sessions. One phone per pair or classroom projection. Optional Bangla action help. No live model calls, real uploads, or collection of personal data. Brand selectors present the same authored simulation in three simplified interface styles; they do not imply real model behavior or expose private reasoning.

## Outcomes
1. Retain ownership of professional claims and correspondence; minimize others' data; disclose AI involvement according to the stated context; assess consequences after publication.
2. Plan, attempt, diagnose, request bounded help, verify against a source, and transfer learning with AI closed. Distinguish fluent recognition from independent understanding.
3. Interrupt unsupported implementation decisions; distinguish symptoms from causes; select adversarial tests; connect architecture, permissions, payment integrity, recovery, and business responsibility to customer outcomes.

## Session architecture
Eight stages per session, 45 minutes each: watch a model, make a prediction, interact with evidence, repair a decision, test transfer, and complete an independent exit check. Pair roles alternate: decision-maker and evidence-checker. English support supplies causal and contrastive frames, not ready-made content answers. No mandatory open-ended typing. Wrong choices reveal a concrete consequence and allow revision; attempts and successful revisions are distinct from first-attempt evidence.

## Required acceptance evidence
- All 24 stages have authored evidence, meaningful actions and explanatory feedback; each session totals 45 minutes.
- Teaching introduction, student deliverables, model demonstration and teacher guidance available.
- Session 1 includes professional correspondence, third-party privacy, publication/image context and honest attribution.
- Session 2 includes an initial independent attempt, bounded tutoring, source verification, a different independent transfer problem and confidence calibration.
- Session 3 includes stepwise stop/continue simulations, a valid continue example, recovery decisions, concrete tests, customer consequences and release/business judgment.
- Mobile and classroom modes; usable keyboard and touch targets; no text hidden beneath fixed chrome; readable forest/cream states.
- Progress survives reload locally; reset is explicit; no live uploads; authored simulation labels visible.
- Test each interaction type and traverse every stage; verify session timings, content, mobile layout and console errors before publishing.

## Sources
Conceptual foundation: Rick Dakan, Joseph Feller and Anthropic, AI Fluency for Students: AI as a Learning Partner; Teaching AI Fluency: The Delegation–Diligence Loop (2025). Supplied transcripts, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Original fictional cases and interactions. Diligence covers creation, transparency and deployment; publication is treated as a deployment decision. Do not teach unverified claims that Claude or ChatGPT secretly watermark ordinary text; accountability does not depend on detection.
