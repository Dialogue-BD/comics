AI Fluency Lab

Own the thinking

Three 45-minute lessons for experienced AI users. Each has eight stages. Use one phone per pair, or project and invite decisions.

Partner A operates the phone. Partner B points to evidence and challenges the decision. Swap at each stage. Before checking, each student explains a reason. At the final stage, decide alone before comparing.

Use the English speaking frame after the action. Bangla support explains the action, not the answer. Success means explaining the evidence and applying the skill to a new problem.

Based on AI Fluency by Rick Dakan, Joseph Feller and Anthropic (2025): delegation, description, discernment and diligence. Adapted from supplied learning-partner and delegation–diligence teaching transcripts, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

All people, outputs and AI work steps are fictional teaching simulations. Interfaces are simplified practice environments, not live products or private model reasoning.
