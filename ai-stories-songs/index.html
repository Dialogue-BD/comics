    AI Tools: Stories & Songs

    Turn your notes into English you want to hear — with Gemini (gemini.google.com)

    ① Make a notebook

    1. In Gemini, tap New notebook.
    2. Add your sources: notes, PDFs, transcripts.

    ② Make a song ♫

    1. New chat → ＋ → Create music.
    2. Pick a card, e.g. Memorize something.
    3. ＋ → More uploads → Notebooks → choose → Add.
    4. Type: I want to memorize ______. ______ style.
    5. Send. Listen. Sing along.

    ③ Make a storybook 📖

    1. New chat → ＋ → More tools → Canvas.
    2. ＋ → More uploads → Notebooks → choose → Add.
    3. Type: Make a storybook about ______ for ______.
    4. Send. Turn the pages. Tap Listen.

    ④ Use the 4Ds

    • Delegation — you choose the sources and the job.
    • Description — say the topic, who it is for, and the style.
    • Discernment — check it against your notes.
    • Diligence — nothing private; say “AI helped me”.
