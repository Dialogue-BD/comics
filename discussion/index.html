How this works

The phone supports the talk. It does not replace it.

Students choose a room, receive a local discussion topic and role, and use the language guide whenever they get stuck. The teacher moves every room through the same discussion phases.

  • No account is needed.
  • No audio is recorded.
  • Names and room work expire with the classroom session.
