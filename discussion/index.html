How this works

The phone supports the talk. It does not replace it.

Students choose a room, receive a local discussion topic, and open the language or inquiry support only when it helps. One shared phone is enough. The teacher sets the broad pace; students facilitate the conversation themselves.

  • No account is needed.
  • No audio is recorded.
  • Names and room work expire with the classroom session.
