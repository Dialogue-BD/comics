# How Are You Feeling? — lesson artwork

Generated for the six-panel guided lesson on 30 September 2026. The files in `img/` are the production assets. The SVG lesson keeps the words, emotion wheel, affective-filter shutter, iceberg rungs, grammar chunks, and timed motion editable and sharp on a projector.

## Art direction and generation prompts

**Shared direction for every asset:** Warm, polished editorial illustration for an English-language classroom in Bangladesh. A contemporary picture-book finish with painterly light, subtle paper texture, restrained detail, and coherent perspective. Deep forest green `#103D21`, muted gold `#B9924F`, warm cream `#F4F1E6`, with clay, plum, and sky-blue accents. Believable Bangladeshi people and dress. No text, logos, speech bubbles, UI, or diagram labels. Morning light from upper left. Images should support the overlaid teaching graphics rather than compete with them.
