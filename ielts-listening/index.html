0:30
Distractor anatomy

The first answer you hear is often bait.

Agency“June to September, is it? That’s what most people want.”
Lily“I don’t want to go on till September. I’d prefer to finish in August.”

Rule: Hold a possible answer lightly. Keep listening until the speaker confirms, corrects, or replaces it.
